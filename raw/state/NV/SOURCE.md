# NV — state education directory sources

Agency: Nevada Department of Education (NDE) — School and District Information; Private Schools

Download date: 2026-10-21

Files are hosted on NDE's CMS upload store (webapp-strapi-paas-prod-nde-001.azurewebsites.net), linked from doe.nv.gov pages (read in headless Chromium).
