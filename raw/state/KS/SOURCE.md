# KS — state education directory sources

Agency: Kansas State Department of Education (KSDE) — Directories

Download date: 2026-10-21

KSDE publishes its directories only as PDFs. The 2025-2026 Kansas Educational Directory is the newest full edition on https://www.ksde.gov/data-and-reporting/directories (no 2026-27 full edition posted yet); the 2026-2027 USD superintendents pictorial directory is the newest superintendent list. Educational Directory: per USD — superintendent (code `S`) and other administrators by role code, district phone/fax/email/homepage, building number; per attendance center — principal name, phone, building email/homepage, enrollment; plus a "Principals in Alphabetical Order" index and sections for **private accredited and private nonaccredited schools**. ~1,751 '@' occurrences (mostly building/district emails). Role codes are explained in the PDF's front matter.
